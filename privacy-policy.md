# Privacy Policy for AFK Journey Data Collection Discord Bot

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 Last Updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot, Roberto, (“the Bot”) collects, stores, uses, and manages screenshots and information submitted by users relating to AFK Journey. The information is collected to support community research, data analysis, and the creation of infographics, guides, and other educational resources for the AFK Journey community.

By using the Bot or submitting information through it, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect the following types of information when you voluntarily submit content:

- Screenshots and images: Images of gameplay, characters, equipment, formations, battle results, game statistics, rankings, and other AFK Journey-related content.
- User-submitted information: Text, statistics, answers to questions, game progress, team compositions, and other information relevant to AFK Journey research.
- Discord information: Your Discord user ID, username, and other basic information necessary to process submissions, associate related data, and manage the Bot's functionality.
- Submission metadata: Submission timestamps, categories, and other information associated with organizing, verifying, or analyzing submitted content.

Please avoid submitting personal information that is unrelated to AFK Journey research. Screenshots may inadvertently contain usernames, chat messages, account identifiers, or other personal information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the Bot is used exclusively for purposes related to AFK Journey community research and content creation, including:

- Creating infographics, charts, statistics, guides, tutorials, and educational resources.
- Analyzing gameplay trends, character performance, team compositions, equipment, and other game-related data.
- Aggregating submissions to identify patterns and develop community resources.
- Verifying, organizing, and improving the accuracy of collected game data.
- Improving the quality and usefulness of future guides and infographics.
- Preventing duplicate, invalid, or misleading submissions and maintaining the integrity of the dataset.

## 3. How We Store and Protect Information

Submitted information is stored in spreadsheets, cloud storage, local files, or other systems used to manage and analyze community data.

We take reasonable measures to protect collected information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no storage system or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to raw submissions may be limited to the Bot's owner, administrators, researchers, or other authorized individuals involved in maintaining the dataset and producing community resources.

## 4. Public Use of Submitted Information

Information submitted through the Bot may be used in publicly distributed infographics, guides, spreadsheets, statistics, and other community resources.

Where reasonably possible, we will use aggregated or anonymized data in public-facing materials. For example, multiple submissions may be combined to produce infographics or guides.

We will share your username as a contributor per our policy within infographics or guides. https://app.notion.com/p/Yaphalla-Guide-Credit-Criteria-30d1ae5f78cf80248991c61a383549a9 

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell users' personal information.

Collected information may be accessed or shared when reasonably necessary for the following purposes:

- Collaborating with trusted contributors or researchers who help analyze data or create community resources.
- Using third-party hosting, storage, database, or infrastructure services required to operate the Bot.
- Responding to valid legal requests or complying with applicable laws.
- Protecting the security, integrity, and legitimate operation of the Bot and its associated projects.

Where practical, information shared for research or content creation will be limited to what is necessary for the relevant task.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We may retain submitted screenshots and information for as long as they remain useful for research, historical comparisons, data verification, and the maintenance of published resources.

You may request the deletion of information associated with your submissions by contacting the Bot's owner or an administrator through the designated Discord support channel.

When submitting a deletion request, please provide enough information for us to locate your submissions, such as your Discord user ID or relevant submission details.

We will review reasonable requests and delete or anonymize identifiable submission records where feasible, subject to legitimate operational needs and applicable legal obligations.

Please note that information already incorporated into published infographics, aggregated statistics, or distributed guides may not be fully removable, particularly when it cannot reasonably be linked back to an individual. Previously downloaded or redistributed materials may also remain outside our control.

## 7. Your Responsibilities

By submitting information through the Bot, you agree that:

- You will submit information voluntarily and to the best of your knowledge accurately.
- You have the right to submit the screenshots and information you provide.
- You will avoid submitting sensitive personal information or unrelated private conversations.
- You understand that submitted data may be analyzed and used to create public AFK Journey community resources.
- You will not knowingly submit malicious files, deceptive information, or content intended to compromise the Bot or its users.

You are encouraged to review screenshots before submitting them to ensure they do not contain information you do not wish to share.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for members of the AFK Journey community and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect sensitive personal information from minors. If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact the Bot's owner or an administrator so that the situation can be reviewed.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Bot's functionality, data collection practices, storage systems, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published in an appropriate location, such as the Bot's Discord server or documentation page. The “Last Updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.

Continued use of the Bot after an updated policy is published indicates acknowledgment of the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
